AWS has launched Nitro Sandboxes, running each opted-in EKS pod, ECS task or EC2 workload in its own Firecracker microVM with a separate kernel, enabled by one runtimeClassName field at no extra charge. A confidential mode adds attestation-gated key release. The documentation lists the trade-offs: no GPU passthrough, at most five sandboxes per vCPU, and half the compute on SMT instances.
